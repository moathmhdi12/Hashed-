About MIC NSW

Sydney renovation specialists you can trust

MIC NSW Pty Ltd is a licensed NSW building contractor delivering kitchen, bathroom and home renovations across Greater Sydney. Every project is managed under one point of contact — from initial concept and design, through trade coordination, to final handover — so you're never left chasing separate contractors or piecing together a scope yourself.

We focus on clear communication, itemised quotations and quality craftsmanship at every stage, using premium materials and finishes chosen to hold up to everyday life. The result is a renovation that's functional, considered and built for long-term value — not just a fresh coat of paint.
