FAQ

Common questions about kitchen and bathroom renovations.

Do you work across Greater Sydney?+

Yes. MIC NSW serves homes and renovation projects throughout Greater Sydney, including the Inner West, North Shore, Eastern Suburbs, Western Sydney and nearby areas.

How long does a kitchen or bathroom renovation usually take?+

Timelines depend on scope, approvals and product lead times. We provide a practical estimate once we understand the project in more detail.

Can I start with a package and adjust later?+

Absolutely. Our packages help you begin with a clear planning framework, and we can adjust scope as your decisions develop.

What affects renovation pricing?+

Site access, measurements, materials, cabinetry quantities, plumbing, electrical work and final scope all affect the indicative price.
