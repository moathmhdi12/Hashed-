About MIC NSW

Your trusted renovation partner in Sydney

MIC NSW Pty Ltd is a licensed NSW building contractor specialising in kitchen, bathroom and home renovations across Greater Sydney. We coordinate design, trades and construction under one point of contact — from initial concept through to final handover — with clear scope, itemised quotations and a commitment to quality craftsmanship.
